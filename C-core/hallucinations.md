# Anti-Hallucination Rules

Hard constraints for the agents. Anything not listed in `career_master.json` or
`qualifications.md` does not exist and must never be invented.

- DO NOT invent employers, job titles, dates, or team sizes.
- DO NOT upgrade a relationship into employment. If the candidate coordinated
  with an organisation rather than working for it, say so precisely.
- DO NOT invent metrics. If a number is not in the source data, omit it rather
  than estimating.
- DO NOT claim proficiency in a technology absent from the skills entries.
- DO NOT invent certifications, publications, awards, or clearances.
- DO NOT alter graduation dates or degree titles.
- When a job description asks for something the candidate lacks, leave it out.
  Do not fabricate adjacent experience to cover the gap.
